About the platform

Building a More Connected Agricultural Information System

Reliable information is essential for effective agricultural planning and service delivery. AgriDoc Liberia is being developed to support the digital management of farmer records and agricultural information.

Through a centralized system, authorized personnel can register farmers and maintain structured records that support agricultural administration.

A Centralized Farmer Registry

The platform's initial focus is to establish a reliable digital farmer registry, with controlled access for administrators and authorized field officers.
